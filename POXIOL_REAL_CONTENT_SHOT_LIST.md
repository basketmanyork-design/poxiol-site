# POXIOL Real Content Shot List

This shot list is designed for the factory/content team. Use real POXIOL products and processes only. Keep the original files and a short source/approval note for every selected photo or video.

## General capture rules

- Photograph in clean, even light without heavy filters or embedded promotional text.
- Capture one horizontal frame for desktop (`3:2` or `4:3`) and one vertical frame for mobile (`4:5`) when practical.
- Keep the complete garment or inspection action in frame; also take separate close-ups.
- Remove customer names, phone numbers, addresses, order documents, shipping labels and barcodes.
- Do not show professional team logos, third-party brands, buyer artwork, employee faces or customer identities without written permission.
- Save the original file, photographer/owner, date, product/project context and public-use approval together.

## Delivery package for website approval

For each delivered asset, include a row containing:

- Proposed asset ID.
- Original filename.
- Photographer/creator and owner.
- Capture date.
- Product or process category.
- Whether it is POXIOL-owned, buyer-authorized, or product-only.
- Exact public-use approval note.
- Visible people, logos, names, artwork or labels and their approval status.
- Suggested page/section.

Do not resize, watermark or add advertising text before delivering the originals. Website-optimized derivatives will be created after approval.
