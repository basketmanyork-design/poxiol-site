The supplied hero MP4 and six PNGs are user-provided campaign/design assets. Their source hashes and derived outputs are in `asset-manifest.json`. They must not be presented as real production, factory, customer or test-order evidence. Existing production/factory pages keep their separate governed asset policy; no new factory image was fabricated for this task. Public form text does not promise a response interval or automatic sample shipment. The approved sample policy in the plan is represented as an eligibility request with confirmation before dispatch.

CMS editors can update the bounded headings and all nine FAQ pairs through the new `sitePage.homepageOptimization` fields after schema deployment. Every commercial claim requires human review before publishing; the code keeps its current approved default when a field is missing or malformed. English copy must be checked against this register before Production publication.
